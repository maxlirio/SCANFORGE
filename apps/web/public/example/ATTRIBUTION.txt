example/model.glb

A 3D model generated by TRELLIS.2 (Microsoft, MIT licence) from a single
photograph, running on Apple Silicon via PyTorch MPS, then re-textured with
SCANFORGE's corrected baker (pipeline/scanforge/trellis_bake.py).

Source photograph: https://github.com/alicevision/dataset_buddha
Licence of the source photograph: CC-BY-4.0 ((c) AliceVision contributors)

The model is a derivative of that photograph and is distributed under
CC-BY-4.0 with attribution to AliceVision. TRELLIS.2 itself is MIT-licensed.
